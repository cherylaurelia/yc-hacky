## Direction contract

THESIS: You write, and someone leans over your shoulder to ask. A warm, editorial page in the spirit of a painter's sketchbook that shows the working product, refusing the dark techno dashboard and the generic SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper #fdfcf6, ink #1f1d18, one deep green #0f4a3f for a mid-page band, focus and selection; amber only for an agent waiting on you. EB Garamond headings at regular weight with one italic phrase; Figtree for everything read or clicked; JetBrains Mono only for commands. Sage dry-brush stroke behind the headline, a torn-paper edge on the one painting, a sage-to-lavender wash framing the product, white lifted sheets for the thing that needs you, a dark ink pill with a waveform, ink buttons, and a giant serif wordmark sinking off the footer.

STORY: In seconds the visitor understands two things: their words land wherever they write, and their agents ask them things that they answer out loud. They believe it is calm and private, and they get the app (web app now, Mac download once released), then pick Pro for voice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav (serif wordmark; Download, Pricing, Docs; Open web app). Left: the headline "Write anywhere. Answer your agents without leaving it." with "Answer your agents" in italic over the sage brush stroke, one support paragraph, then the primary action (before the first release: "Try the web app" with "Mac app coming soon"; after: "Download for Mac" with "Free · macOS 13.3 or later"). Right: ter Borch's Curiosity cropped to the writer and the woman leaning over her, with a torn edge and a one-line credit. The product stage follows immediately below on its wash.

FORM: editorial split hero plus the split-desk product stage (signature interaction kept: the waiting agent's question crosses into the pill, fn goes down, the answer is sent, then dictation types into the draft). seed: none (pinned) "use simialr design style liek this https://www.heyaristotle.com/about", "also inspiration from wispr flow https://wisprflow.ai", "https://willowvoice.com take inspo from thsi too"
