Set up Terminal 1

Terminal 1 talks to you about your coding agents. Chrome needs your OK once before the side panel can hear you.

  1. Start the app: in your yc-hacky checkout, run npm run dev.
  2. Click the Terminal 1 icon in the toolbar for the queue, or press Alt+Shift+Space to open the side panel and talk.
  3. Pin the icon: the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to Terminal 1.

The app address and repository are in the side panel’s settings.
